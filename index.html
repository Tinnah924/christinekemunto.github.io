About Me

I’m a Swahili Translator, Localization Specialist, and Telecommunications & IT Specialist passionate about making technology accessible across languages and communities.


My work combines language expertise with a technical background, allowing me to translate and localize complex subjects while maintaining their meaning, terminology, and technical accuracy.


I have contributed to Swahili translation and localization projects covering Bitcoin, technology, finance, and digital education. I also participate in language-building initiatives and technology communities that promote access to information in African languages.
